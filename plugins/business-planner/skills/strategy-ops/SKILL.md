# Strategy-Ops

The planner produces a decision; this skill produces what the founder **runs on**. Three documents (templates in `references/strategy-ops-templates.md`): Strategy Doc, Operating Plan, Investment Card.

## Time is scenarios, not simulation

Simulating a business years forward produces confident fiction — compounding uncertainty defeats role-play (a documented LLM failure mode). The honest tool is **scenario planning**: a few named futures, pre-committed responses, and tripwires. This builds the forward-looking muscle a founder needs *without* demanding a 10-year plan before they start. Most great businesses started without one; scenarios are rehearsal, not homework.

- **Four scenarios, always named:** **Base** (plan roughly works) · **Squeeze** (slower/thinner: costs +20%, sales −30%, payments late) · **Breakout** (demand outruns plan — capacity, cash, and quality strain) · **Kill** (the core assumption fails).
- Per scenario: earliest **leading indicators**, the **pre-committed response** ("if CAC > ₹X by month 6 → pause paid, shift to channel Y"), and what it does to cash.
- **Tripwires are the output that matters:** specific, measurable, dated. Include the tells from competition-sim as monitors. Review them at the operating cadence — this is how "long term" enters a founder's week without paralysis.
- Horizons: 0–90 days (operate) · 3–18 months (scenarios active) · 18 months+ (directional bets only — one paragraph, revisited quarterly, never detailed).

## Strategy Doc — the Rumelt kernel

Good strategy is not goals or ambition; it's **Diagnosis → Guiding Policy → Coherent Actions** (Rumelt, `frameworks.md`):
1. **Diagnosis** — the one or two critical factors that define this situation (from the plan: the real constraint, the real opening).
2. **Guiding policy** — the approach that turns the diagnosis into advantage; what we will *not* do (pull from non-goals + hard DON'Ts).
3. **Coherent actions** — 3–7 moves that reinforce each other, sequenced (competition-sim's heat map informs sequencing: quiet niches first).
Add: moat trajectory (Seven Powers lens — what compounds), the scenario table, and the tripwire list. Strategic frameworks (Porter, PESTLE, Ansoff) apply **only where they bite** — one framework that explains the situation beats five decorating it.

## Operating Plan — the next 90 days

- **3 objectives max** (OKR-lite): outcome-phrased, each with 1–3 measurable key results tied to plan numbers.
- **Actions with owners and dates** — in a 1–3 person business most owners are the founder; that's fine, the calendar-honesty is the point (check against founder-fit's time budget: an over-committed plan fails silently).
- **Cadence:** daily (cash + orders glance) · weekly (numbers vs plan, 30 min) · monthly (tripwire + Stories Ledger review) · quarterly (strategy + scenario refresh).
- **The constraint** (Theory of Constraints): name the single current bottleneck; the plan's center of gravity is relieving it.
- Kill/stop numbers carried forward from the plan — visible, not buried.

## Investment Card — makes ideas comparable

One standard card per idea (template in references). Money in (staged) · founder time in (hrs/wk × months, priced at their real alternative wage) · payback months · **NPV** at the founder's true discount rate (gold-loan/FD+ for MSME, opportunity cost of salary, or venture hurdle — never a textbook 10%) · **IRR** for base/bear/bull · owner earnings Y1/Y3 by scenario · max drawdown (worst cash hole) · reversibility % (recoverable if killed at month 6) · the call.

**Anti-precision-theater rule:** every number shows its 2–3 driving assumptions and appears as a three-scenario range. A single confident NPV on a pre-revenue plan is fiction with decimals. The card's purpose is *comparison and honesty* — two ideas, two cards, side by side — not prediction. Re-verify any market figure by search before it enters the card (planner rule 6).

## Flow position

Runs at **Phase 5 (COMMIT)** of the planner's flow — after the plan has passed the review gates and the customer/competition sims, and iterations have settled (2–3 loops max; more is polishing fiction). Can also run standalone on an existing business ("give me an operating plan for what I already run"). Outputs feed the plan template's final sections and stand alone as run-documents the founder reviews on cadence.
